Dear Cristina,

I am Joo Lee, editor of SANKIT — a ski, hiking and outdoor magazine published in Korean on Instagram and Threads.

I am writing to ask whether SANKIT could be added to your press list for Salewa.

Three things would help us most:

1. Press releases for new products and seasonal collections
2. Product images we may use in articles
3. Korean release dates and Korean retail prices, where they exist

On images, please attach them to an email where possible — JPG or PNG, 2000px or wider is plenty, and a ZIP is fine for larger sets. Links that require a login or expire after a period are difficult for us to work with. If you tell us the model name, colourway, the credit line to print and the permitted use, we will follow it exactly.

What we commit to in return:

- We print model names, Korean prices and official points of sale accurately.
- We name the Korean importer or distributor and tag the official account.
- If you tell us something is wrong, we check it and publish a correction.
- We never crop, retouch or overlay text on a press image when the licence requires it unmodified.

SANKIT began in November 2026, so we are new and still small. I started it because good gear reaches Korea without the story behind it reaching readers — what the makers were solving, and who the product is actually for. That is the gap we are trying to close, and we intend to be at it for a long time.

If there is a Korean distributor who handles press for Salewa, I would be glad to be pointed to them instead.

Our address for press material is sankitmag@gmail.com.

Thank you for your time, and I hope the season goes well for you.
